The working notes are included verbatim (one file-name reference to the build specification in `Phase5_architecture.md` is replaced by the words "the build specification") as the record of what was specified, claimed and tested. They contain first-person text from the time and refer to documents that are not in this repository (see `docs/research-history.md`). The statuses they assign ([EST], [EMP], [CONJ], [PCV]) are the author's working tags, not publication claims.
