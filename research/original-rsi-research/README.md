# Original RSI study

*Bayesian Inference in Random Transposition Shuffles*, Mihika Singhania, under the direction of Dr Arvind Ayyer (Department of Mathematics, Indian Institute of Science), Research Science Initiative India, 9 July 2026.

## Contents

* `paper/RSI_Paper_Mihika_Singhania.pdf` — the paper as it exists (28 pages including appendices and supplementary figures).
* `figures/` — figure files found in the author's working folder `RSI figures`: PNG exports (`1.png` and `n=512, m=32, R=1000.png` are renderings of the four-panel sampling-distribution figure; `2.png` is a rendering of the minimum-sample-size figure with values that differ from the published Figure 9, see `docs/known-issues.md` D5; `posterior_step6.png`, `system size, n.png`, `512_mgaussianlikelihood/*.png`), PDF exports (`gaussianlikelihood.pdf`, `likelihood_family_theta.pdf`, `step9_mstar_scaling.pdf`) and `calibration plots.pptx`. File names are the author's own. No script is stored with them; the corresponding code is described in `experiments/original-research/README.md`.

## Not included

Programme administrative material found next to the paper (milestone reports, the first milestone presentation, mentor and tutor feedback, a mini-paper with comments, a temperature-chart page): these are RSI-programme documents with comments from third parties and are not research outputs of this repository. They are listed in `docs/inventory.md`.
